GRAN'S MAYO

Credit: Janice Richardson

Ingredients

Gran's Mayo ingredients
Hard-boiled egg yolks2 (whites for salad)
Sugar4 tbsp
Mustard1 tsp
Salt & pepperto taste
Cream1 cup (or more)
Brown vinegar2 tbsp

Method

  1. Hard boil 2 eggs, then peel and separate. Reserve the whites for a salad — this recipe only uses the yolks.
  2. In a bowl, mash the egg yolks, sugar, mustard, salt and pepper together with a fork until smooth.
  3. Add the cream (start with 1 cup, add more for a thinner dressing) and the brown vinegar.
  4. Mix well until creamy and combined.
  5. Transfer to a clean jar. Keeps in the fridge for 2–3 weeks.

Notes

Generations of family favourites
