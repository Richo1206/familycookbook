GRAN'S TOMATO RELISH

Credit: Janice Richardson

Ingredients

Gran's Tomato Relish ingredients
Base 1.5x
Tomatoes, chopped3 kg4½ kg
Onions, chopped1 kg1½ kg
Salt2 tbsp3 tbsp
Sugar1 kg1½ kg
Curry powder2 tbsp3 tbsp
Mustard powder3 tbsp4½ tbsp
Cayenne pepper1 tspheaped tsp
Ground ginger1 tspheaped tsp
White vinegar2 cups3 cups

Method

  1. Sprinkle salt over chopped tomatoes and onions, mix through thoroughly, and stand overnight.
  2. Next morning, pour off the liquid.
  3. In a large pot, boil together the sugar, curry powder, mustard powder, cayenne pepper, ginger, and white vinegar for 10 minutes.
  4. Add the drained tomatoes and onions and boil for 1 hour, stirring occasionally.
  5. Put the mixture through a moulee using a large-size strainer.
  6. Bottle into sterilised bottles while hot.
  7. Sterilise jars separately before bottling.
  8. Warm jars for 15 minutes at 110°C, then reduce to 50°C and keep warm until needed.
