ALI'S MING LING

Credit: Alison Richardson

Ingredients

Ali's Ming Ling ingredients
Minced beef1 kg
Onion, diced1 small
Beef stock3 cups
Rice1½ cups
Chicken noodle soup1 packet
Sweet chilli sauce2–3 tbsp
Curry powder2 tsp
Frozen peas & corn1 cup
Cabbage, chopped½

Method

  1. Heat a large pan over medium heat. Fry the diced onion until soft and translucent.
  2. Add the minced beef and cook, breaking it up as it browns, until cooked through and well chopped.
  3. Pour in the beef stock and bring to a simmer.
  4. Stir in the rice, chicken noodle soup packet, sweet chilli sauce and curry powder.
  5. Add the frozen peas, corn and chopped cabbage and stir to combine.
  6. Cover and simmer for about 15 minutes, or until the rice is cooked and most of the liquid has been absorbed. Stir occasionally so it doesn't catch on the bottom.
