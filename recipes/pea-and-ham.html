PEA & HAM SOUP

Credit: Janice Richardson

Ingredients

Pea and Ham Soup ingredients
Green split peas500 g packet
Brown onions, chopped2
Carrots, peeled & chopped3
Celery sticks, chopped3
Garlic cloves, chopped2
Ham hock1 large (~750 g)
Water12 cups
Chicken stock cube1
Salt & black pepperto taste
Frozen peas (optional)handful, to finish

Method

  1. Follow the cooking directions on the back of the split pea packet (usually a soak or rinse step, depending on the brand).
  2. Optional: heat a tablespoon of oil in a large pot and fry the chopped onion and garlic until soft.
  3. Add the split peas, carrot, celery, ham hock, water, chicken stock cube, salt and pepper to the pot.
  4. Bring to the boil, then reduce to a low simmer. Leave to simmer for around 2 hours, until the peas are completely soft and the ham is falling off the bone.
  5. Remove the ham hock. Shred the meat off the bone and set aside. Discard the bone, skin and fat.
  6. Let the soup cool slightly, then blend smooth with a stick blender (bamix). Return the shredded ham to the pot.
  7. When re-heating to serve, optionally stir in a handful of frozen peas for fresh colour and texture.
